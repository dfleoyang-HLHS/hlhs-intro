    COMPETENCIES

    HLHS Six Competencies × 6Cs

    The “Six Competencies” of HLHS’s student profile correspond to the 6Cs of global competencies promoted by international Deep Learning (NPDL). Among them, the Learning competency corresponds to Collaboration, and the Practice competency corresponds to Citizenship.

    Why the Six Competencies and the 6Cs?

    “Nurturing the next generation with strong citizenship who can solve real-world problems” is the mission of HLHS. The six competencies express what we hope our students will become, while the 6Cs give us a shared language with the world, so teachers know exactly which competency they are developing when they design a course.

    VisionTake the world as your calling; enrich humanity through books and poetry.

    Five Learning Dimensions of Citizenship

    Taking “Citizenship” as an example, HLHS sets a deep-learning progression that both teachers and students can refer to.

    Dimension 1

    Having a global perspective

    Students look at: engagement with issues

    Dimension 2

    Working for human equality and well-being through empathy and compassion

    Students look at: empathy toward diverse cultures

    Dimension 3

    Holding a sincere interest in the sustainability of humanity and the environment

    Students look at: understanding of environmental sustainability

    Dimension 4

    Solving ambiguous and complex problems in the real world

    Students look at: confidence in problem-solving tasks

    Dimension 5

    Using technology to learn about related issues

    Students look at: level of digital information literacy

      School-based Featured Course · Hualien Studies

      Starting from the White Lighthouse, get to know the land beneath our feet.

      Year 10Learning stage
      4 creditsTotal credits

      Course groups

      • A　School history and local development history; a first look at works by alumni writers
      • B　Healthy and happy living; cultivating information logic
      • C　Aesthetic education and English poetry writing

      Field visits: the north route visits Egushi Ryosaburo Memorial Park, and the south route visits the 228 Peace Memorial Park.

      Taught by nine subject areas together

      Hualien Studies is held in Year 10 every Thursday, periods 3 and 4. Teachers of history, biology, Chinese, English, art, music, geography, information technology and home economics take part together, helping students get to know Hualien from many different fields.
